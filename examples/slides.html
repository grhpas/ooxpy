Tutorial 10: Layouts, tables, shapes and notes in PowerPoint

Office's standard layouts, tables in built-in styles, shapes with fills, speaker notes, picture placeholders, reading a slide back, editing tables and links. Script: examples/slides.py

Standard layouts

from oox import PresentationDocument

deck = PresentationDocument.new()
print(len(deck.layout_names), deck.layout_names[2:5])
slide = deck.add_slide("Two Content")
deck.set_placeholder_text(slide, "title", "Plan")
deck.set_placeholder_text(slide, 1, ["Research", (1, "interviews")])
deck.set_placeholder_text(slide, 2, ["Build", "Launch"])
print([(ph.type, ph.idx) for ph in deck.placeholders(slide)])
11 ['Section Header', 'Two Content', 'Comparison']
[('title', 0), ('obj', 1), ('obj', 2)]

A new deck has Office's eleven layouts with Office's own placeholder positions, scaled for the slide size: Title Slide, Title and Content, Section Header, Two Content, Comparison, Title Only, Blank, Content with Caption, Picture with Caption and the two vertical-text layouts. PresentationDocument.new(layouts="basic") gives only Title Slide, Title and Content and Blank. Placeholders are addressed by index when a layout has several of the same kind.

Tables

from oox.units import Inches

slide = deck.add_slide("Title Only")
deck.set_placeholder_text(slide, "title", "Results")
deck.add_table(slide, [["Region", "Q1", "Q2"], ["North", 120, 135], ["South", 95, 110],
                       ["Total", None, 245]],
               x=Inches(1), y=Inches(2), width=Inches(8), merges=["A4:B4"],
               style="Medium Style 2 - Accent 1")
info = deck.tables(slide)[0]
print(info.rows[3], info.merges, info.style_name)
['Total', '', '245'] ['A4:B4'] Medium Style 2 - Accent 1

Tables use PowerPoint's built-in styles, by name (oox.slides.TABLE_STYLES) or by GUID. header=, banded= and first_column= switch the style's options, as on PowerPoint's Table Design tab. Merges use spreadsheet-style ranges: columns A, B, … and rows 1, 2, ….

Speaker notes

deck.set_notes(slide, "Mention that totals are rounded.")
print(deck.notes(slide))
Mention that totals are rounded.

The first note creates the notes master, as PowerPoint does. notes() reads notes from any deck and returns None for a slide without notes.

Shapes

slide = deck.add_slide("Blank")
deck.add_shape(slide, "roundRect", x=Inches(1), y=Inches(2), width=Inches(3), height=Inches(1.2),
               text="Plan")
deck.add_shape(slide, "chevron", x=Inches(4.5), y=Inches(2), width=Inches(3), height=Inches(1.2),
               text="Build", fill="ED7D31")
deck.add_shape(slide, "ellipse", x=Inches(8), y=Inches(2), width=Inches(2), height=Inches(2),
               text="Done", fill="70AD47", line="375623", line_width=2)
print([(s.kind, s.geometry, s.text) for s in deck.shapes(slide)])
[('shape', 'roundRect', 'Plan'), ('shape', 'chevron', 'Build'), ('shape', 'ellipse', 'Done')]

Shapes: rect, roundRect, ellipse, triangle, diamond, hexagon, octagon, the four arrows, homePlate and chevron. Fill and line take hex colours or None; text is centred and white unless you say otherwise.

Picture placeholders

slide = deck.add_slide("Picture with Caption")
deck.set_placeholder_text(slide, "title", "The team")
deck.set_placeholder_text(slide, 2, "Offsite, May 2026")
deck.set_placeholder_picture(slide, "pic", "chart.png")
print([s.kind for s in deck.shapes(slide)])
deck.save("plan.pptx")
['placeholder', 'picture', 'placeholder']

The picture takes the placeholder's place and position, cropped to fill it the way PowerPoint does (crop=False stretches it instead). Content placeholders take pictures too. shapes() lists what any slide holds: placeholders, text boxes, shapes, pictures, tables, charts and groups, with their text.

Editing a table

from oox import open_document

deck = open_document("plan.pptx")
results = deck.slides[1]
table = deck.table(results)            # the slide's first table; or by shape name
table.set_text(1, 1, 125)              # keeps the cell's font and size
table.add_row(["West", 80, 84], index=3)
table.add_column(["Q3", 150, 99, 91, None])
table.delete_row(2)                    # South
print(table.values())
print(table.merges())
[['Region', 'Q1', 'Q2', 'Q3'], ['North', '125', '135', '150'], ['West', '80', '84', '91'], ['Total', None, '245', '']]
[(3, 0, 3, 1)]

deck.table() edits any table: one made by oox, by PowerPoint or by python-pptx. Rows and columns are 0-based. New rows and columns copy the formatting of their neighbour, the frame grows with them, and merges shrink when a row or column through them goes; inserting inside a merge is refused. merge(), unmerge(), set_widths() and set_style() do the rest.

Links

slide = deck.add_slide("Title Only")
deck.set_placeholder_text(slide, "title", "Next steps")
box = deck.add_text_box(slide, "Details in the wiki; questions to the team.", x=Inches(1),
                        y=Inches(2), width=Inches(8), height=Inches(1))
deck.set_link(slide, box, "https://wiki.example.org/falcon", text="the wiki")
deck.set_link(slide, box, "mailto:falcon@example.org", text="the team")
arrow = deck.add_shape(slide, "leftArrow", x=Inches(1), y=Inches(4), width=Inches(2),
                       height=Inches(1), text="Results")
deck.set_link(slide, arrow, results)   # jump to a slide in the show
for link in deck.links(slide):
    print(link.text, link.kind, link.target or link.anchor)
deck.save("plan.pptx")
the wiki url https://wiki.example.org/falcon
the team url mailto:falcon@example.org
None slide 2

A link goes on text (text=, split out of its run if needed) or on the whole shape. Targets are URLs, slides, or the show jumps "next", "previous", "first", "last" and "end"; None removes a link. links() reads them from any deck.
